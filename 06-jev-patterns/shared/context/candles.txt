Identify the pattern ending at the right edge. Green closes at or above its open; red closes below. Body means absolute close minus open. Long/short compares with the previous ten bodies; tiny wick means less than 10% of the previous ten ranges. Doji: body at most 10% of those ranges. Hammer: short body, lower wick longer than body, tiny upper wick; body bottom near the preceding low (within 20% of the five ranges preceding that candle), following a decline. Shooting Star: short body, upper wick longer than body, tiny lower wick, body gaps above the preceding body, following a rise. Bullish/Bearish Engulfing: green/red body envelops the opposite-color preceding body; at least one endpoint extends strictly. Morning/Evening Star: long red/green body, short body gapped below/above, then a non-short opposite-color body closes over 30% into the first body. Stars require decline/rise. Trend is the five-session close return before the pattern starts. None: no listed pattern; overlapping patterns are excluded.
